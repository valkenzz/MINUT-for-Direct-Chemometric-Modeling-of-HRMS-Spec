# COVID-19 severity metabolomics: literature table (21 studies)

Plasma and serum metabolites reported as associated with COVID-19 status or severity in the 21 studies listed
below (bibliographic records checked against Crossref and Europe PMC; each entry taken from the full text and
traced to a Results paragraph, table or figure; compiled on 5 November 2026). Section 9 of `covid.ipynb` reads
the table to decide which candidates of the preliminary list (`covid_literature_candidates_prelim.csv`) are
named, with a direction, in the literature; the rows kept here are those that qualify at least one candidate.
Axes: **POS** = COVID-19⁺ vs COVID-19⁻/healthy; **SEV** = severe/critical/ICU/fatal vs mild/moderate/non-severe
(within COVID-19⁺); **OUT** = deceased vs discharged.
